Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Purpose

This application is a personal-use Google Drive synchronization tool used by the site owner through rclone. It is not offered as a public service.

Google user data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files and related file metadata that the owner has authorized rclone to access.

How data is used

Google Drive data is used only to synchronize the owner's files between Google Drive and the owner's local computer and backup systems.

Storage

Copies of synchronized files may be stored on the owner's local systems and backups. OAuth credentials used by rclone are stored on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, transferred, or shared with third parties by this application, except as necessary for Google and rclone to provide the synchronization functionality requested by the owner.

Retention and deletion

Files and backups are retained or deleted by the owner as part of their personal file-management and backup practices.

Access and control

The owner can revoke this application's access to Google Drive at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to the contact email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
